# Ball teams — shared-ball composition in the Players step

Status: **accepted** (2026-08-01). Extends
[format-templates.md](format-templates.md); does not change its decisions.

Related:

- [ADR-0003: Balls are subjects; formats rank a chosen set of balls](../adr/0003-balls-as-subjects.md)
- [ADR-0004: Sides are subjects; any ball format ranks a side](../adr/0004-sides-as-subjects.md)

## Context

Two gaps motivated this:

1. **iOS cannot create teams at all.** `CreateDraftBuilder`'s contract is
   "no hand-built teams"; the only teams it emits are `multi_ball` sides
   auto-derived from format preset cards. A four-player group that wants to
   play two scramble pairs — stroke play *and* match play on the same two
   team-balls — cannot set that up on iOS, and on web only through the
   flexible form.
2. **The flexible form's team-kind labels are incomprehensible.** "One
   combined ball" vs "Separate balls (a side)" exists because one `TeamForm`
   entity serves two unrelated concepts, distinguished by a `kind` toggle
   the user must understand.

The scenario above is not a missing template. A template ships with a
*format* (format-templates.md §1); "scramble pairs" is not a format — it is
a **composition** that changes who produces balls, after which any ball
format ranks those balls (ADR-0003). Making "Scramble stroke play" and
"Scramble match play" cards would be per-combination cards — the exact
north-star violation.

## The model: two kinds of team, two surfaces

| | `single_ball` (ball team) | `multi_ball` (side) |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Players sharing **one physical ball** (scramble, foursomes, greensomes) | Players on **own balls**, scored together by a format |
| Shape depends on | Nothing — pairing is format-independent | The format (Taliban = 2×2, …) |
| Created in | **Players step** — optional "ball teams" section | **Format step** — generated by game cards, adjusted per game |
| Kind toggle shown | Never — implied by surface | Never — implied by surface |

Nobody in the mainline ever answers "plays as?". The web flexible editor
keeps the select for exotic setups (nesting, custom formations), relabelled
(§6). Teams remain **round-level and shared between games**
(format-templates.md §3): pair up once, and stroke play + match play both
rank the same two team-balls.

Downstream consequences:

- Format card **eligibility derives from the resulting ball roster**
  (team-balls + remaining individuals), not the raw player count. 4 players
  as 2 scramble pairs → 2 balls → match play (2 balls) and stroke play both
  light up; Taliban (2 sides × 2 own-ball players) does not.
- Per-game ball assignment (format-templates.md §4) operates on that ball
  roster; a ball team moves as one unit and cannot be split across a game's
  balls.
- Leftover players stay individual balls — 5 players as 2 pairs + 1 solo is
  legal (3 balls). Sit-out rules are unchanged.

## Formations are rule objects, not metadata

`formation` was pure metadata. It becomes a descriptor:

```ts
interface FormationDescriptor {
    id: 'scramble' | 'foursomes' | 'greensomes';
    label: { en: string; sv: string };
    /** Team size bounds. Foursomes/greensomes are exactly 2. */
    size: { min: number; max: number };
    /** Default allowance % by position, members sorted by playing
     *  handicap ascending (lowest first). */
    allowances: (memberCount: number) => number[];
}
```

`custom` stays a valid draft `formation` value (web flexible editor only);
it has no descriptor — unbounded size, manual percentages.

### Default allowances (established conventions, WHS/USGA)

Position 1 = lowest playing handicap.

| Formation | Size | Allowances by position |
|---|---|---|
| Foursomes | exactly 2 | 50 / 50 |
| Greensomes | exactly 2 | 60 / 40 |
| Scramble | 2 | 35 / 15 |
| Scramble | 3 | 30 / 20 / 10 |
| Scramble | 4 | 25 / 20 / 15 / 10 |
| Scramble | 5–8 | 25 / 20 / 15 / 10, then 5, then 0-floor (no convention exists; a documented default, freely overridable) |

### Seeding semantics

- **Defaults are computed, overrides are sticky.** Seeding sorts members by
  playing handicap and applies the recipe. Membership or handicap change →
  re-seed, *unless* the team carries a manual override (per-team
  `customized` flag); then the user's numbers are kept.
- Overrides are edited **per player**; position only determines defaults.
- **Snapshot at play time is untouched** — percentages freeze into the
  draft (`allowancePct` per member) and then into `ball_players` as always.
  Re-seeding only ever happens during setup.
- Formation bounds are enforced in the pairing UI (foursomes refuses a 3rd
  member), and scramble is capped at 8 (under `MAX_TEAM_SIZE = 10`).
- Per-team formation, defaulting new teams to the last-chosen formation —
  no extra concept, covers "everyone plays scramble".

## Server: `/setup/formations`

A new `formationCatalog()` in `server/domain/round-setup/` (serializable
descriptors: `allowances` ships as the per-size table, not a function),
exposed as `GET /setup/formations` in `setup.api.ts` beside
`/setup/formats`, unauthenticated like the rest of the SELECT side. Both
clients read one source; adding a formation never touches client code.

**The draft schema does not change.** `DraftRoundTeam` already carries
`formation` + per-member `allowancePct`; the builder never needs the
recipe. No migration, no new persistence.

The server does not validate formation size bounds in v1 — the draft's
existing team-size validation stands; bounds are a setup-UI concern like
the rest of the seeding.

## UI

### Players step — the ball teams section (iOS + web)

Optional, collapsed by default, below the roster:

> **Playing scramble or foursomes?**
> Group players who share one ball. Skip this if everyone plays their own
> ball.
> `[ Set up teams ]`

Opened:

- **Formation chips**: Scramble / Foursomes / Greensomes (3 options —
  within the chips rule). The words golfers already know are the labels.
- **Pairing**: tap players into teams; bounds per formation. Unassigned
  players remain individual balls.
- **Team row states the consequence in plain words**:
  *"Anna + Marcus · Scramble · plays one ball · HCP 9"* — combined playing
  handicap = `round(Σ memberCH × pct%)`, live.
- **Allowance override**: per-member % pre-filled from the recipe,
  editable; editing sets `customized`.

Swedish helper text: *"Spelar ni scramble eller foursome? Gruppera spelare
som delar en boll."* Formation names stay untranslated (used as-is in
Swedish golf).

### Web flexible editor relabel

The kind select stays for exotic setups, relabelled to behaviour:

- "Share one ball (scramble, foursomes)"
- "Own ball each, scored together as a team"

("side" is banned user-facing jargon and must not appear.)

## iOS scope

`CreateStore` gains roster-level ball-team state (members, formation,
overrides); `CreateDraftBuilder` drops the "no hand-built teams"
restriction for `single_ball` teams: emits them in `teams[]`, includes them
as `team` subjects in ball formats, excludes their members from individual
subjects (the double-scoring trap, format-templates.md §4 — must be
test-covered), and computes card eligibility from the ball roster.
`EditDraftAssembler` round-trips them. `FormatCatalog` eligibility switches
from player count to ball count.

Out of iOS scope (web flexible editor remains the escape hatch): team
naming, nested teams, `custom` formation.

## Phasing

Hand-test at each gate.

| Phase | Work | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| A | `formationCatalog()` + `GET /setup/formations` + tests; regenerate clients | Catalog served; recipes match the table above |
| B | iOS: store + draft builder + edit assembler + unit tests (no UI) | Draft with 2 scramble pairs + stroke play + match play builds and passes preflight; double-scoring test |
| C | iOS: Players-step section UI, eligibility from ball count | The 4-player scenario playable end-to-end from the phone |
| D | Web: Players-step section, seeding, relabels, eligibility from ball count; single_ball teams owned by the section leave the flexible Teams list | Same scenario on web without opening the flexible form |

## Out of scope

- Formation-aware server validation of team sizes.
- Scramble drive-minimums, greensomes tee rules or any play-rule
  enforcement — Tapscore scores; it does not referee.
- Saving compositions ("same pairs as last week").
- Team naming (labels stay positional `Team A/B` everywhere).

## Open questions

- Whether the format step should visibly group a ball team's members in
  score entry order / playing groups (a single-ball team already warns when
  split across groups).
- Whether `custom` formation should eventually surface on iOS at all.
